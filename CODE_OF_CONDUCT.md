# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This repository is intended to foster respectful, evidence-based discussion surrounding cybersecurity, digital investigations, and online fraud awareness.

We welcome contributions from learners, professionals, researchers, and enthusiasts regardless of background or experience.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants are encouraged to:

- Communicate respectfully.
- Support claims with evidence.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Share knowledge generously.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Maintain professional discussions.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not permitted:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Doxxing or sharing personal information
- Encouraging illegal activities
- Publishing confidential material
- Spreading misinformation intentionally
- Spam or promotional abuse

---

## Responsible Disclosure

If you identify an issue that could affect someone's privacy or security, please avoid posting sensitive details publicly. Instead, open a private discussion where appropriate.

---

## Enforcement

Contributors who repeatedly violate this Code of Conduct may have their contributions rejected or removed to maintain a constructive learning environment.

---

## Acknowledgement

By participating in this repository, you agree to uphold these standards and contribute to a respectful, educational environment.
