Overview

This repository documents an independent cybersecurity investigation into a cryptocurrency investment opportunity introduced through Telegram and associated with RunaAsset ("runace.com").

The investigation examined the platform's public-facing information, domain registration details, available business records, and the communication patterns surrounding the investment opportunity.

Rather than evaluating investment performance, the objective was to assess the platform's transparency, examine potentially concerning indicators, and determine whether its claims and business identity could be independently verified using the available evidence.

The case study combines communication records available to the investigator with publicly accessible information and supporting research.

Investigation Objectives

The investigation sought to answer the following questions:

- Could the platform's ownership and corporate identity be independently established?
- Was verifiable regulatory information available?
- Did the communication contain indicators associated with social engineering?
- Could the investment-related claims be independently substantiated?
- What risks should a potential participant consider before engaging with the platform?

The investigation was limited to information and records lawfully available to the me.

Methodology

The investigation followed a structured process:

1. Initial Contact Documentation — Recording the circumstances surrounding the Telegram interaction.
2. Conversation Analysis — Reviewing relevant messages, claims, referral activity, and communication patterns.
3. Website Inspection — Examining the platform's public-facing content, transparency, and available organizational information.
4. Domain Registration Analysis — Reviewing available WHOIS/RDAP information for "runace.com".
5. Business Registry Research — Examining available business records for a similarly named entity.
6. Evidence Correlation — Comparing observations across communication records, website information, and external sources.
7. Risk Assessment — Evaluating the significance and limitations of the observed indicators.
8. Final Documentation — Organizing findings, supporting exhibits, and recommendations into a case study report.

Key Findings

The investigation identified several indicators that warranted caution:

- Ownership transparency: The platform's ownership could not be independently established from the information reviewed.
- Regulatory transparency: Sufficient verifiable regulatory information was not established during the investigation.
- Referral-based introduction: The investment opportunity was introduced through a referral, making the recruitment and communication process relevant to the assessment.
- Business verification: A similarly named Maryland-registered business was identified. However, no verified relationship between that business and "runace.com" was established.
- Communication patterns: Certain aspects of the interaction warranted examination against commonly recognized social engineering indicators.
- Investment claims: The available information was insufficient to independently substantiate the investment-related claims.

These observations should be interpreted within the scope of the available evidence. They do not independently establish fraud, criminal activity, or regulatory violations.

Investigation Conclusion

The investigation concluded that the platform presented unresolved transparency and verification concerns.

The available evidence was insufficient to establish the platform's ownership, independently substantiate its investment claims, or verify its relationship with the similarly named registered business.

Consequently, the opportunity was assessed as unverified and suspicious, and engaging financially without further independent verification was not recommended.

This is an evidence-based risk assessment, not a definitive determination of fraud or illegality.

Ethical Considerations and Limitations

This investigation was conducted for educational, research, and cybersecurity awareness purposes.

Important limitations:

- Findings reflect the evidence available and reviewed during the investigation.
- Domain privacy does not, by itself, establish malicious intent.
- Similar business names do not establish corporate affiliation.
- The absence of information in a particular public database does not automatically establish illegality.
- Reconstructed exhibits are distinguished from original evidence.
- No definitive accusation of criminal activity is made against any individual or organization.

Readers should independently verify relevant information before making financial or security-related decisions.
