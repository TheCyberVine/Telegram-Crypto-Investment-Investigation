## Overview

This repository documents an independent cybersecurity investigation into a cryptocurrency investment opportunity introduced through Telegram.

Rather than evaluating investment performance, the investigation focused on identifying indicators commonly associated with online investment fraud through ethical Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT), behavioral analysis, and publicly available information.

The objective was to determine whether the platform demonstrated sufficient transparency, verifiable corporate identity, and trustworthy operational practices.

---

## Objectives

The investigation sought to answer the following questions:

- Can the platform's ownership be independently verified?
- Does the organization provide transparent regulatory information?
- Are the communication methods consistent with known social engineering techniques?
- Does publicly available evidence support the investment claims?
- What level of risk would an informed user reasonably assign?

## Key Findings

The investigation identified several indicators requiring caution, including:

- Limited ownership transparency
- Unverified regulatory information
- Referral-based recruitment
- Limited corporate verification
- Communication patterns consistent with common social engineering techniques

The report does **not** conclude that criminal activity has occurred. Rather, it presents an evidence-based risk assessment derived from publicly observable information.
