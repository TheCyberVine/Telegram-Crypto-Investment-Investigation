References and External Resources

This directory documents the external resources relevant to the investigation of the Telegram-based cryptocurrency investment opportunity associated with RunaAsset ("runace.com").

The resources below provide independent reference points for domain-registration checks, business-record verification, and investment-related due diligence.

1. Domain Registration and WHOIS/RDAP

- "ICANN Lookup" (https://lookup.icann.org/) — Search domain registration information.
- "ICANN Registration Data Access Protocol (RDAP)" (https://www.icann.org/rdap) — Information about the RDAP system used to access domain registration data.

Relevance: These resources can help verify publicly available domain-registration information, including registrar details, registration dates, and available registrant information.

Limitation: Privacy-protected registration information does not, by itself, establish fraudulent activity or malicious intent.

2. Business Registration Verification

- "Maryland Business Express" (https://businessexpress.maryland.gov/) — Official Maryland business search and registration services.

Relevance: Used as a reference point for examining the similarly named Maryland-registered business identified during the investigation.

Limitation: A similar business name does not establish ownership, operation, endorsement, or affiliation with "runace.com". No such connection should be assumed without independent evidence.

3. Investment and Financial Regulatory Checks

- "U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)" (https://www.sec.gov/) — Official information about securities regulation and investor protection.
- "SEC Investment Adviser Public Disclosure (IAPD)" (https://adviserinfo.sec.gov/) — Search registered investment advisers and relevant public disclosures.
- "Investor.gov" (https://www.investor.gov/) — Investor education and information about investment-related risks and fraud indicators.

Relevance: These resources provide reference points for understanding investment regulation and checking relevant registration information where applicable.

Limitation: The absence of a result in one regulatory database does not automatically prove that an entity is operating illegally. Regulatory requirements depend on the entity's activities and jurisdiction.

4. Primary Investigation Evidence

The following materials form the primary evidence base for this case study:

- Redacted Telegram conversation screenshots.
- Redacted investment referral message.
- Screenshot of the RunaAsset website homepage.
- WHOIS/RDAP lookup result.
- Business registry search result.
- Investigation timeline reconstructed from available records.

These materials should be reviewed alongside the investigation report and its methodology.

5. Evidence Handling and Limitations

The investigation relied on available communications, publicly accessible website information, domain-registration data, and business-record research.

The following distinctions are important:

- Observations are not automatically proof of wrongdoing.
- A similarly named registered business is not necessarily connected to the investigated website.
- Domain privacy is not proof of malicious intent.
- Website claims that could not be independently verified remain unverified.
- Reconstructed exhibits must not be presented as original screenshots captured during the initial investigation.

The investigation's conclusion should therefore be understood as a risk assessment based on the evidence available, not a legal determination of fraud or illegality.
