# Purpose

This document explains the methodology used during the investigation of a cryptocurrency investment opportunity introduced through Telegram.

The objective was to evaluate the platform using ethical Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) techniques and publicly available information while maintaining accuracy, transparency, and professional investigative standards.

---

# Investigation Objectives

The investigation aimed to answer the following questions:

- Can the platform's ownership be independently verified?
- Is the business identity transparent?
- Are regulatory or licensing claims publicly verifiable?
- Do the communication patterns exhibit characteristics commonly associated with social engineering?
- Based on available evidence, what level of risk should be assigned?

---

# Investigation Principles

The investigation followed five guiding principles.

## 1. Evidence Before Conclusions

Conclusions were based only on observations supported by documented evidence.

No assumptions were treated as facts.

---

## 2. Public Information Only

Only publicly accessible information was examined.

No attempt was made to obtain unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or confidential information.

---

## 3. Independent Verification

Whenever possible, claims made during conversations or on the website were compared against independent public sources.

---

## 4. Documentation

Evidence was documented throughout the investigation to preserve context and maintain consistency between observations and findings.

---

## 5. Professional Neutrality

The investigation focused on assessing observable risk indicators rather than proving or disproving criminal activity.

---

# Investigation Scope

## Included

- Telegram conversation analysis
- Website inspection
- WHOIS research
- Public business verification
- Transparency assessment
- Behavioral analysis
- Risk assessment

## Excluded

- Penetration testing
- Reverse engineering
- Financial transactions
- Login attempts
- Malware analysis
- Exploitation of systems
- Collection of private information

# Investigation Stages

## Stage 1 – Initial Observation

The initial conversation was documented without making assumptions regarding legitimacy.

The objective during this stage was to preserve context and identify potential investigative leads.

---

## Stage 2 – Website Inspection

The website was reviewed to evaluate:

- Ownership transparency
- Company information
- Contact information
- Platform claims
- General trust indicators

---

## Stage 3 – Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)

Publicly available information was used to verify observable claims.

Examples include:

- WHOIS domain information
- Public business records
- Public corporate information
- Website content

---

## Stage 4 – Behavioral Analysis

Communication patterns were analyzed to identify observable behaviors such as:

- Rapport building
- Authority references
- Referral-based promotion
- Responses to verification questions
