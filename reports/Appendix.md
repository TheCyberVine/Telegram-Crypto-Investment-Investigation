# Appendix D — Glossary

### Behavioral Analysis

The study of observable communication patterns and interactions.

---

### Corporate Transparency

The degree to which an organization openly provides verifiable information about its identity, ownership, and operations.

---

### OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence)

The process of collecting and analyzing information from publicly available sources.

---

### Risk Assessment

The process of evaluating the likelihood and potential impact of identified risks.

---

### Social Engineering

Techniques that attempt to influence people into making decisions or revealing information through psychological manipulation rather than technical exploitation.

---

### WHOIS

A public registration system that provides information about domain names, subject to registrar policies and privacy protections.

# Appendix E — Investigation Limitations

The following limitations should be considered when interpreting the findings.

- Only publicly available information was examined.
- Internal company records were not accessible.
- The investigation did not involve financial transactions.
- No penetration testing or system exploitation was performed.
- Some ownership information may be legitimately protected by privacy services.

Accordingly, the findings represent an evidence-based assessment rather than definitive proof regarding the platform's legitimacy.

---

# Appendix F — Privacy and Redactions

To protect privacy and promote responsible disclosure:

- Personal names were removed where appropriate.
- Usernames were redacted where appropriate.
- Referral codes were omitted.
- Wallet addresses were partially redacted where appropriate.
- Private conversations were included only to the extent necessary to support the investigation.
