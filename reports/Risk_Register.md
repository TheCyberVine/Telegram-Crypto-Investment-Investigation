# Risk Register

## Telegram Cryptocurrency Investment Investigation

**Author:** Divine Kylian  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Category:** Cybersecurity | Risk Assessment

---

# Purpose

This document summarizes the principal risks identified during the investigation.

The register is intended to provide a structured overview of observed risk indicators, their potential impact, and recommended mitigation measures.

A risk listed in this document should **not** be interpreted as proof of fraudulent activity. It represents an assessment based on publicly observable information collected during the investigation.

---

# Risk Rating Scale

| Likelihood | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| Low | Unlikely based on available evidence |
| Medium | Possible but not strongly supported |
| High | Multiple indicators suggest increased concern |

| Impact | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Low | Limited potential consequences |
| Medium | Moderate financial or security consequences |
| High | Significant financial, privacy, or trust consequences |

Overall Risk is determined by considering both likelihood and potential impact.

# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Evidence | Likelihood | Impact | Overall Risk | Recommended Mitigation |
|----|------|----------|------------|--------|--------------|------------------------|
| R-01 | Limited ownership transparency | Domain ownership could not be independently verified through publicly available information. | High | High | **High** | Verify ownership using independent sources before engaging. |
| R-02 | Unverified regulatory information | No independently verifiable regulatory or licensing information was identified during the investigation. | High | High | **High** | Confirm registration with the relevant regulatory authority where applicable. |
| R-03 | Referral-driven promotion | The opportunity was introduced through a referral during a private conversation. | Medium | Medium | **Medium** | Treat referral-based investment opportunities with additional scrutiny. |
| R-04 | Communication patterns associated with social engineering | Rapport building and authority references were observed before the investment opportunity was introduced. | Medium | High | **High** | Verify claims independently and avoid relying solely on personal recommendations. |
| R-05 | Limited corporate transparency | Publicly available information did not clearly establish the relationship between the platform and the referenced company. | High | High | **High** | Seek independent confirmation of corporate identity before investing. |
| R-06 | Information asymmetry | A prospective investor could not easily verify key claims using publicly available information alone. | Medium | Medium | **Medium** | Request supporting documentation and compare it with independent sources. |
